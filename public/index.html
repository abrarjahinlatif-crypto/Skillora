WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Help & support

Quick answers for your learning journey.

How does a roadmap work?

Enter a subject, choose your current level and weekly study time. The AI creates levels with lessons, practice tasks, quizzes and resources.

How do I earn progress?

Open a lesson, study the notes, complete its in-app practice task and pass the level quiz. Progress is saved to your account.

Can I download my roadmap?

Yes. Open a roadmap and choose Export PDF. You get a one-page flowchart of your whole journey. In the print window, choose Save as PDF or print it on paper.

Why is there a daily limit?

AI generation has a daily limit to keep usage fair and operating costs predictable. The default is 10 roadmaps per day.
